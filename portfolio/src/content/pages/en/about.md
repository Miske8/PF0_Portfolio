## My background

I am a web and application developer, trained in both the fundamentals of the web — semantic HTML, CSS, JavaScript — and in server-side technologies. My journey started with curiosity: understanding how a page is built, then how a service is designed, deployed and maintained over time.

Across projects, I have learned to work across the whole chain: scoping the need with the client or Product Owner, designing the interface, building the front-end, writing APIs, modelling data, then deploying and monitoring.

## My technical profile

I work mainly with **JavaScript and TypeScript**, on both the client and the server. I favour frameworks that prioritise simplicity and performance — Astro for content sites, React for application interfaces, Node.js and Express for APIs.

Three things get my particular attention:

- **Accessibility**: a website must be usable by everyone, with a keyboard or a screen reader.
- **Performance**: every kilobyte and every request costs the user something.
- **Maintainability**: readable code today is changeable code tomorrow.

## As a person

I am rigorous and autonomous, but above all I believe in communication. A project goes well when expectations are explicit: I would rather ask a question early than deliver a feature that misses the need. I enjoy teamwork, code reviews and discussions where a solution is challenged to find a better one.

Outside of code, I am interested in ergonomics, photography and technical watch — three areas that feed into how I design interfaces.

## What I am looking for

I want to invest in projects where I can have a real impact on the product, work with demanding people and keep growing technically. Freelance missions, product-team roles and long-term collaborations interest me equally.
